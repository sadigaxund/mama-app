# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Board with one tab per stage, order counts, and red or green cards by order kind.
- Order form to create and edit orders, with a garment type list that grows from the form.
- Orders move with the card arrow or a long-press menu, and every move can be undone for 4 seconds.
- Order detail screen with payments, expenses and the balance due, plus edit and delete.
- Phone numbers can be picked from contacts, and the call button opens the dialer.
- Search by name, phone or order number, and filter by kind or garment type.
- Settings for language (Russian, Azerbaijani, English), currency and reminder defaults.
- Photos from the camera or gallery on each order, shown on the card and in a full-screen viewer.
- Visits (fittings, pickups) per order, with a prompt to book one after moving to a fitting or Done.
- Morning reminders for upcoming and overdue deadlines, with Done, Move deadline and Stop actions, and a reminder before each visit.
- Visit reminders can go off at several times before a visit, chosen in Settings.
- Share an order as text, an image or a PDF bill through any app.
- Daily backup of orders and photos to Google Drive, with restore on a new phone.
